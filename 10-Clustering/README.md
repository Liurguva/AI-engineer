# Chapter 10 — Nearest Neighbors and Clustering

- `10.KNN_leo.py` and `10.KNN_sklearn.py`: supervised k-nearest-neighbor classification.
- `10.Kmeans_leo.py` and `10.Kmeans_sklearn.py`: K-means clustering.
- `10.DBSCAN_leo.py` and `10.DBSCAN_sklearn.py`: density-based clustering.
- `10.GMM_leo.py` and `10.GMM_sklearn.py`: Gaussian-mixture clustering.
